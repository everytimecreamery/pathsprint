# Phase 5: Tone of Voice Guidelines

- **Empathetic & Relatable:** We understand the anxiety of growing up in a vernacular-speaking household and facing English interviews.
- **Direct & Action-Oriented:** No academic fluff or motivational quotes. Every session gives tangible templates, frameworks, and homework.
- **Supportive & Non-Elitist:** Never shame anyone for pronunciation errors or broken sentences. Focus on clarity and confidence.
