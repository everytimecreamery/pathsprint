# Phase 5: Brand Identity — Mission, Vision & Core Values

## Mission
To empower ambitious Tier-2 and Tier-3 college graduates to break through career confusion, master professional communication, and build credible proof-of-work so they can enter the corporate workforce with pride.

## Vision
A future where a student's career potential is determined by their practical capability, communication clarity, and proof-of-work—not the postal code or tier of their college.

## Core Values
1. **Radical Transparency:** Zero false placement promises. We tell students the honest truth about ATS filters, recruitment realities, and skill requirements.
2. **Capability Over Credentials:** Working code, real portfolio case studies, and clear spoken thought beat shiny certificates every single time.
3. **Safe-Space Practice:** A judgment-free laboratory where mistakes in practice pods are celebrated as stepping stones to fluency.
4. **Radical Affordability:** Premium quality coaching accessible for less than the cost of a college textbook (₹499–₹1,499). No predatory student loans.
