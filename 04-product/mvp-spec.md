# Phase 6: MVP Operational Specification & Pod Protocol

## The 4-Student Micro-Pod Protocol
- **Matching:** Students matched by target domain (Tech, Data, Finance, Marketing) and English comfort level.
- **Duration:** 60 Minutes on Google Meet.
- **Structure:**
  - Student A speaks for 3 minutes (e.g., "Tell me about yourself" or STAR project answer).
  - Students B, C, D provide structured positive & constructive peer feedback (2 mins).
  - Rotation repeats for all 4 students (35 mins total).
  - Founder provides 15 mins of expert synthesis, grammar polish, and delivery coaching.
- **Benefit:** 75% reduction in founder live teaching hours compared to 1:1 coaching, while completely eliminating student stage fright.
