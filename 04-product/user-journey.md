# Phase 6: Student User Journey Map

1. **Awareness:** Discovers a LinkedIn resume teardown video or joins a college WhatsApp group link.
2. **First Touch:** Attends the Free Sunday 90-min Masterclass on ATS hacks & HR communication.
3. **Onboarding:** Enrolls in the 30-Day Sprint (₹999 early-bird / ₹1,499 regular) via Razorpay Payment Page; completes intake form.
4. **Sprint Execution:** Receives Loom resume critique (Day 3); joins 4-person practice pod for live drills (Week 2); completes LinkedIn optimization (Week 3); clears mock HR interview (Week 4).
5. **Graduation & Advocacy:** Earns readiness badge; posts before/after transformation on LinkedIn; becomes a paid Campus Ambassador.
