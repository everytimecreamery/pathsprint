# Phase 6: Product MVP Definition — 30-Day Placement Sprint

The MVP is a 30-day cohort-based accelerator blending asynchronous video audits, live peer micro-pods, and 1:1 capstone mock interviews.

## 4 Weekly Sprints
- **Week 1: Diagnostic & ATS Re-engineering:** Diagnostic intake form, live ATS masterclass, 1-page resume build, 7-min personalized Loom video audit.
- **Week 2: STAR Spoken English Pods:** Frameworks for articulation; 2x live 4-student practice pods drilling 90-second intros and pressure questions.
- **Week 3: Proof-of-Work & LinkedIn Inbound:** LinkedIn profile overhaul, replacing tutorial clones with 2 verified real-world business case studies.
- **Week 4: Group Discussions & Capstone Mock HR:** Full cohort GD simulation; individual 25-minute mock HR interviews with graded scorecards.
