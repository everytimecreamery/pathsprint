# Phase 3: Market Research & TAM/SAM/SOM Analysis

## Core Macro Statistics (AISHE & ISR 2024–2025)
- **Higher Education Enrollment:** 43.3 Million students enrolled in Indian colleges and universities [AISHE].
- **Annual Graduating Pool:** 9.5M to 10.2M graduates entering the labor force each year.
- **Overall Graduate Employability:** 54.81% in 2025 (India Skills Report 2025 / CII).
- **The Employability Deficit:** 45.19% (~4.5 Million fresh graduates per year) are unemployable without supplementary training.
- **Stream Breakdown:**
  - B.Tech / Engineering: 71.5% employable
  - MBA: 78.0% employable
  - B.Sc / Science: 58.0% employable
  - B.Com / B.A. (Generalist degrees): Under 45% employable

## Market Sizing
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** 12 Million students and unplaced graduates = **₹1,800 Crore (~$215M USD)** at ₹1,500 blended ARPU.
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** 3.5 Million Tier-2/3 undergraduate job-seekers = **₹420 Crore (~$50M USD)**.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM - Year 1):** 1,200 to 1,700 students = **₹17L to ₹24L ARR ($20k–$28k USD)** via organic masterclasses and campus ambassador referral networks.
