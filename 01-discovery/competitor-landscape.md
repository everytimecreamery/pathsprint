# Phase 3: Competitor Landscape & White Space Analysis

| Competitor | Pricing Tier | Core Offering | Key Vulnerability / Strategic White Space |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preplaced** | ₹10,000 – ₹45,000 | 1:1 tech interview prep with FAANG mentors | Prohibitively expensive for Tier-2/3; tech-only focus |
| **Topmate.io** | ₹300 – ₹2,500 / call | Marketplace for 1:1 booking | Purely transactional; no structured curriculum, no community |
| **Seekho** | ₹99 – ₹999 | Recorded vernacular video lessons | Passive content; sub-10% completion; no live speaking practice |
| **Unstop** | Free (B2B hiring model) | Campus hackathons & contests | Filters the top 5% talent; leaves behind the 60% who struggle |
| **Clapingo** | ₹1,500 – ₹4,000 / mo | 1:1 spoken English tutoring | Disconnected from job search; no ATS resumes or technical projects |
| **PathSprint** | **₹499 – ₹1,499** | **Live Pods + ATS Loom + Proof-of-Work** | **White Space: Accessible, peer-accountable corporate readiness** |
