## 1. Regulatory & Legal Clearance (CRITICAL TAILWIND)
- **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) & ASCI Statutory Guidelines (Nov 2024):** Strict nationwide ban against misleading EdTech claims, unverified "100% placement guarantees", and inflated average CTC promises, backed by penalties up to ₹50 Lakhs.
- **PathSprint Strategic Alignment:** PathSprint explicitly positions on *practical capability, spoken confidence, ATS optimization, and project proof-of-work* rather than guaranteed jobs. This removes 100% of regulatory exposure while exploiting massive student distrust of predatory coaching centers.
- **Licensing:** Operates as an online training/mentorship platform with upfront digital fees; no UGC/AICTE university accreditations required.

## 2. Dominant Incumbents & The "₹1,000 Chasm"
- **Bootcamps & High-Ticket Programs (Scaler, UpGrad, Preplaced):** Charge ₹15,000–₹1,50,000. They focus predominantly on Tier-1/Tier-2 elite engineering roles and ISA/NBFC debt models.
- **Low-Cost Mass EdTech (Seekho, Internshala):** Charge ₹99–₹999 for pre-recorded asynchronous videos. Result: sub-10% completion rates, zero speaking practice, zero personalized attention.
- **The White Space:** The ₹500 to ₹1,500 price bracket for live, peer-driven accountability pods and personalized ATS feedback is wide open.

## 3. Precedent Failure Analysis (FrontRow Post-Mortem)
- **Case:** FrontRow raised $18M+ from Lightspeed/Elevation and shut down in July 2023.
- **Root Cause:** Hobby learning ("learn comedy, gaming, singing from celebrities") collapsed in India because willingness-to-pay is exclusively tied to career and earning outcomes.
- **Key Takeaway for PathSprint:** Do not position as abstract "self-improvement" or "personality development". Every single session must directly improve job search velocity: passing ATS resume filters, getting interview shortlists, and clearing HR rounds.
