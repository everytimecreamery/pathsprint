# Phase 2: Idea Exploration & Strategic Brainstorming

## 5 Operating Model Variations
1. **30-Day Cohort Sprints (Selected Core):** Fixed start dates, 4-student live practice pods, weekly deliverables. Cuts founder live delivery hours by 75% while boosting completion rates to >75%.
2. **Loom-First Career Clinic (Selected Hybrid Element):** Asynchronous 7-minute personalized video critiques of resumes and elevator pitches. Removes 100% of calendar scheduling friction.
3. **B2B2C College TPO Booster:** Partnering with Tier-2/3 college placement cells for flat ₹25k–₹50k campus workshops. High volume, zero student CAC, but longer sales cycles (best for Phase 2 expansion).
4. **Proof-of-Work Project Guild:** 21-day guided sprints dedicated to building 2 verifiable business case studies. Solves recruiter complaints about generic tutorial clones.
5. **Reverse-Hiring Graduate Showcase:** Curated talent directory where startups pay ₹10k–₹25k per successful hire. Long-term monetization strategy once brand authority is established.

## Optimal Hybrid Architecture
A hybrid of **Variation 1 (Cohort Pods)** and **Variation 2 (Asynchronous Loom Audits)**, keeping founder delivery under 27 hours/month for 25 students while maintaining an effective hourly wage of ₹1,225/hr.
