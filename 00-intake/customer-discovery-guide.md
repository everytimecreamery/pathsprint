# Phase 3.7: Customer Discovery Guide (Mom Test Protocols)

## Key Student Interview Questions (Past Behavior Focus)
1. *"When was the last time you applied for an internship or job? What exact steps did you take?"*
2. *"How many applications did you send, and how many responses did you get?"*
3. *"When you think about an HR interview, what specific moment makes you feel most anxious?"*
4. *"Have you ever spent money on courses, resume writers, or English apps? How much did it cost, and what happened?"*
5. *"What does your college placement cell do for you right now, and where does it fall short?"*

## Red Flags & Golden Signals
- **Red Flag (False Positive):** "Yes! I would definitely pay ₹1,500 if someone helps me get placed!" (Hypothetical commitment).
- **Golden Signal:** "I applied to 80 companies on LinkedIn and got 0 replies, so I paid ₹500 to an online resume reviewer on Instagram last month, but the resume still got rejected by ATS." (Past active spending to solve the exact pain).
