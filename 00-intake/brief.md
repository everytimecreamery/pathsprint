# Phase 1: Intake Brief & Operational Scope

**Project:** PathSprint (Career Clarity Hub)  
**Target Audience:** Tier-2/3 Indian Undergrads (3rd/Final Year) & Unplaced Freshers  
**Core Purpose:** Bridge the academic-to-corporate employability gap without false placement promises.

---

## The 7 Operational Pillars
1. **Career Clarity & Diagnostic:** Structured assessments across Tech, Data, Finance, Marketing, and Operations.
2. **Professional Identity & Personal Branding:** 1-page ATS resumes, LinkedIn profile makeover, GitHub/Notion portfolio.
3. **Spoken English & Communication:** 4-student practice micro-pods drilling 90-second introductions, STAR behavioral answers, and spontaneous conversation.
4. **Practical Proof-of-Work Projects:** Hands-on domain case studies replacing generic tutorial clones (ToDo/Weather apps).
5. **1:1 Industry Mentorship:** Dedicated 60-min sessions with working corporate professionals (charged separately at ₹799–₹1,499).
6. **Interview Readiness & Group Discussions:** GD moderation drills, HR answering frameworks, and individual 25-minute mock HR interviews.
7. **Accountability Community:** WhatsApp/Discord peer network with daily voice-note streaks and mutual progress monitoring.

## The Solo Founder Labor Trap (Mitigated)
- **The Risk:** Delivering pure 1:1 sessions for resume writing, English coaching, and project guidance at ₹1,499 consumes 3.5–4 hours of founder labor per student. At 20 students, that equals 80 hours of manual fulfillment for just ₹29,980 (~₹375/hour).
- **The Structural Fix:** Shift from pure 1:1 fulfillment to **4-student peer practice pods** and **asynchronous 7-minute Loom video resume audits**.
