# Phase 4: Value Proposition Canvas

## Customer Profile
- **Jobs to be Done:** Pass resume ATS screeners, speak confidently in HR screening calls, clear technical interview rounds, build a credible portfolio.
- **Pains:** Being ghosted after hundreds of applications; extreme anxiety speaking English to authority figures; feeling unprepared despite a college degree.
- **Gains:** Getting interview calls; feeling calm and articulate on camera; having tangible project evidence to discuss.

## Value Map
- **Products & Services:** 30-Day Placement & Confidence Sprint, 4-student speaking pods, personalized Loom resume audits.
- **Pain Relievers:** 1-page ATS formatting guarantees machine readability; safe-space peer pods eliminate fear of judgment.
- **Gain Creators:** Practical case study blueprints replace generic tutorial clones; mock HR interviews provide real readiness scores.
