# Phase 4: Business Model Architecture

## Pricing Tiers & Unit Economics
- **Tier 1: Career Foundation (₹499)** — Asynchronous ATS resume review + LinkedIn profile checklist + 1 revision. Fulfillment: 15 mins.
- **Tier 2: Career Confidence Sprint (₹1,499)** — Flagship 30-day cohort with 4-student speaking pods, Loom audit, and capstone mock HR interview.
- **Tier 3: Career Accelerator (₹2,499)** — Everything in Tier 2 + 1:1 dedicated 60-minute industry mentor advisory session.

## 25-Student Sprint Cohort Economics
- **Gross Revenue:** 25 students × ₹1,499 = **₹37,475 INR**
- **Direct Costs:** Payment gateway (₹750) + Referral rewards (₹2,000) + Software tools (₹2,500) = **₹5,250 INR**
- **Net Gross Profit:** **₹32,225 INR (86.0% Net Margin)**
- **Founder Delivery Labor:** Pods (12h) + Masterclasses (3h) + Loom audits (3h) + Mock HR (8.3h) = **26.3 Hours/Month**
- **Effective Founder Hourly Wage:** ₹32,225 / 26.3 hours = **₹1,225 / Hour (~$14.75 USD/hr)**
