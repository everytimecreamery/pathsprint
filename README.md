# PathSprint — Online Career Development & Mentorship Community

> **"We don't promise jobs. We build confidence, clarity, and capability."**  
> *Tagline: Build Skills. Build Confidence. Build Your Future.*

---

## Executive Overview
**PathSprint** is a modern, student-centric career development ecosystem designed for college undergraduates (3rd & final year) and unplaced fresh graduates in India. It bridges the critical divide between rote academic degrees and modern corporate hiring demands through personalized guidance, ATS-compliant personal branding, spoken English practice pods, proof-of-work project guidance, and dedicated 1:1 industry mentorship.
