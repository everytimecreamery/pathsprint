## 2. Core Risk Matrix & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Placement Expectation Mismatch** (Students demand jobs despite disclaimers) | High | High | Bold, prominent onboarding agreements: *"We do not sell jobs; we build competence."* Measure progress in quantifiable metrics (Interview shortlists, ATS score). |
| **2. Founder Burnout (1:1 Service Trap)** | High | Critical | Enforce 4-student practice micro-pods. Limit 1:1 live calls strictly to Mock Interviews and Mentorship calls. |
| **3. High Drop-off in Practice Sessions** | Medium | Medium | Accountability deposit: Students gain completion badges and community perks only upon attending all pod sessions. |
| **4. Low Mentor Retention** | Medium | Medium | Standardized 60-min booking slots, ₹600–₹850 payouts, and curated high-intent students who have completed foundational homework. |

---

## 3. Kill Criteria (When to Pivot or Halt)

1. **Pre-Pilot Conversion Failure:** If 2 free Sunday Masterclasses with 100+ total attendees yield fewer than 5 paid sign-ups (<5% conversion) after addressing pricing objections.
2. **Delivery Dissatisfaction:** If student NPS drops below 40 or >25% of students drop out before completing their speaking pod sessions.
3. **Founder Unit Economics Breakdown:** If time tracking shows delivery exceeds 4 hours per ₹1,499 package and students reject pod/async formats.
