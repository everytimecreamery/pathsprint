## 2. Pricing Economics: The Founder Time Trap & Resolution

### The Trap (Pure 1:1 Delivery)
If the founder delivers all communication practice and reviews 1:1:
- Career Confidence (₹1,499): 3 x 30-min speaking sessions (1.5h) + 1 mock interview (45m) + resume/LinkedIn teardown (1h) = **3.25 hours**.
- **Effective Hourly Rate:** ₹1,499 / 3.25h = **~₹461/hour gross**.
- **Max Monthly Capacity:** At 25 hours/week of 1:1 calls, max students = ~30 students/mo = **₹44,970 gross revenue**. The business hits a hard revenue ceiling.

### The Resolution (The Micro-Pod & Async Model)
- **Speaking Practice:** 4 students per 45-minute live pod facilitated by a timer and prompt cards. 1 founder hour now covers 4 students (Founder time per student drops to **11 minutes**).
- **Resume Teardown:** 7-minute personalized Loom screen recording instead of a 45-minute live call.
- **Result:** Founder delivery time per Career Confidence student drops from 3.25 hours to **1.1 hours**.
- **Effective Hourly Rate:** ₹1,499 / 1.1h = **₹1,362/hour**. Capacity quadruples to 80–100 students/month with zero burnout.
